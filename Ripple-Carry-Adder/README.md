# 4-Bit Ripple Carry Adder Using Verilog HDL

## Introduction

A Ripple Carry Adder (RCA) is one of the most fundamental arithmetic circuits used in digital electronics. It performs the addition of two binary numbers by connecting multiple Full Adders in series. The carry generated by each Full Adder is passed to the next stage, causing the carry to "ripple" through the circuit until the final sum is produced.

Due to its simple structure and ease of implementation, the Ripple Carry Adder is widely used for learning digital design concepts and serves as a building block for more advanced arithmetic circuits in processors, microcontrollers, and FPGA-based systems.

This project implements a **4-bit Ripple Carry Adder** using Verilog HDL and verifies its functionality through simulation and testbench analysis.

## Theory

A Full Adder adds three input bits:

- A (First Input Bit)
- B (Second Input Bit)
- Cin (Carry Input)

It produces:

- Sum
- Cout (Carry Output)

### Full Adder Equations

**Sum = A ⊕ B ⊕ Cin**

**Cout = (A & B) | (B & Cin) | (A & Cin)**

In a 4-bit Ripple Carry Adder, four Full Adders are connected in series. The carry output from one stage becomes the carry input for the next stage.
